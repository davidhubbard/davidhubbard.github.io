# A Little Lower Than the Angels

Is there anything more thrilling than the cause of Christ?

Psalm 8:

**1** O LORD our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth! who hath set thy glory above the heavens.

**2** Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength ...

**3** When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;

**4** What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?

**5** For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour.

**6** Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet.

### What if I'm waiting on the Lord?

Jesus Christ's call could be the answer you are looking for?

“By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.” (John 13:34)

Don't be afraid of rejection. Don't be afraid of the human condition, your weakness, anyone's
criticism except God's opinion of you.

"And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made
perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my
infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me." (2 Corinthians 12:9)

[What if I'm in trouble with Christ for what I made?](2025-09-17-disciple.html)

Have you ever felt so much love that time slows down for a moment? Has Christ's
beautiful love changed the course of your life for the better?

What if it was always meant to happen in this way? What if every moment led you
to find Him? I think this is a hopeful thought.

### What if my congregation is opposed to you?

**Definitely, counsel with your leader.** This is a charitable cause, and
good scrutiny is necessary before you jump in.

As Jesus said, "Come and see." (John 1:38-39)

If you feel like contributing in any way, head over to
[the discussion](https://github.com/davidhubbard/davidhubbard.github.io/discussions)

This is exciting work. While we build, we know that half of what we build (or
more) will end up redone later.

I like to compare physics and engineering to faith. Even if the comparison is
mismatched, the symmetry is striking.

### I definitely don't agree with what you're doing. What should I do?

Please be respectful of the community we are building. We're doing it as an
expression of love.

### What about another project?

The projects here are the ones that best fit our team. Please feel liberated
to work on a project that you are most passionate about!
